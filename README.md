# 🏛️ Pocket Pantheon

A tiny god simulator in a single HTML file. Twelve founders wash up on a procedural island; you hold the sky.

**▶ Play it here: https://tgrede.github.io/pocket-pantheon/**

Raise a civilization from huts and hunger: villagers are born with heritable traits, pair off, argue, deepen their bonds or split and migrate, learn Crop Rotation or Bronze, and pave dirt roads to one another's doors — while you decide what the hand from the sky is for.

- **The Hand** — pick people up (gently, or not), raise and lower them, throw them
- **Powers** — Smite, Bless, Rain, Meteor, Feast, Blight, and Life itself, all on a mana budget
- **The Chronicle Book** — every soul's lineage, traits, grudges and gifts, and every village's story
- Villages can *deepen* instead of splitting: a Commons Lodge, clan bonds, and roads between the houses

No build, no server, no dependencies — the whole game (engine included) lives in `index.html`. Your world saves in your own browser.
